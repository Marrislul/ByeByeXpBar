**Bye Bye XPBar** removes the XP bar.

I use it together with a different XP tracker addon, just pick one you like.

- **Retail:** the whole status bar goes away, so the reputation bar is gone too.
- **WoW Forever:** only the XP bar goes away, your reputation bar stays.

**Why does this addon exist?**

I made a UI for my max level main and wanted to use it on my alts as well. But they had the XP bar right on the first action bar.
